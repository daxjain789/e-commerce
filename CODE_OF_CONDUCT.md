# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute without being made to feel
unwelcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, generous about people.
- Assume competence and good faith; ask before assuming intent.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect that maintainers have final say on scope and direction.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory jokes or slurs, sexual attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and sustained disruption of discussions are not tolerated — in issues, pull
requests, commit messages or any project space.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the maintainer at **conduct@meridiansupply.co**. Reports are read
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or ban participants
who violate this policy.

This is a condensed version of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org)
v2.1.
